RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. TERM OF LEASE
This lease is for a period of 12 months, commencing on January 1, 2024, and ending on December 31, 2024.

2. RENT
The Tenant agrees to pay $1,500 per month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
A security deposit of $1,500 is required. It is fully refundable at the end of the lease, provided there is no damage beyond normal wear and tear.

4. MAINTENANCE
The Landlord is responsible for all major repairs. The Tenant is responsible for minor maintenance such as changing lightbulbs.

5. TERMINATION
The Tenant must provide 30 days' written notice before terminating the lease.

6. LATE FEES
A late fee of $50 will be charged for any rent received after the 5th of the month.
