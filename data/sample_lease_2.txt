RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. TERM OF LEASE
The lease duration is 6 months.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant must pay a non-refundable security deposit of $3,000 before moving in.

3. RENT INCREASE
The Landlord reserves the right to increase the rent at any time without prior notice.

4. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs, including plumbing and structural issues, regardless of cause.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this lease without cause at any time, requiring the Tenant to vacate within 7 days.

6. SUBLETTING
Subletting or use of the property for Airbnb is strictly prohibited. Violation will result in immediate eviction.
